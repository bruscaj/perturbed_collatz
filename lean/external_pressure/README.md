# Finite word dictionaries and actual pressure helpers

This package contains 30 finite word-level results and 34 analytic helper results for log(3)/log(2). They establish actual uniform phase pressure, compact-tilt convergence, exact physical/source transports, reference/cost normalization and cofinal integrated lower brackets. ARGUMENT.md gives the conventions and derivations; target_map.json maps each alias to its frozen goal and proof.

This package requires its separate source-pinned independent assurance gates. At introduction its full Linux assurance was pending; exact CI receipts determine later acceptance. Full M4/R8/R9, baseline/infinite-tilt bounds, all-threshold entropy/Legendre, critical regularity, effective programs and the certified table remain incomplete.

The 63 original mathematical modules are exact byte copies; the 64-target wrapper and audit are new. The 77 model-origin entries preserve current raw types/values, including 17 opaque null bodies. Historical WORD values are retained separately and never substituted for nulls. Historical Draft/UNFROZEN comments are provenance; specification.json records the later freezes.
